## Description

**Treadmill Mayhem** is a wobbly, physics-packed party race on a treadmill that never stops. Run, bump and boost to stay on the belt. Last can standing wins!

**RACE MODE**
- Six racers on one treadmill. The belt gets faster every few seconds.
- Shove rivals off the back, grab stamina orbs, and fire SUPER SPEED when it's charged.
- Five AI difficulties, from Easy to Insane.

**OBSTACLE MODE**
- Dodge crates, rolling bars, walls and falling hail.
- 12 handcrafted levels, plus an endless Free Play that never stops escalating.
- Earn Gears for Obstacle-only upgrades: a longer belt, a wider belt, extra lives, and a gear magnet.

**12 PLAYABLE CHARACTERS**
- Play as the Tuna, a bouncy Ball, a slippery Fish, a bulldozing Rolling Pin, the Paper Towel, the Onion, and more.
- Each one handles differently.

**UPGRADE AND DECORATE**
- Earn Tuna Coins every run and upgrade speed, stamina, balance, boost and more.
- Deck out the arena with a disco ball, lasers, a camera drone, neon signs and confetti cannons.

**COME BACK FOR MORE**
- Daily rewards, achievements, and offline earnings while you're away.

**Fair by design:** no energy timers and no paywalls. Every upgrade can be earned by playing.

## App Privacy ("nutrition label")
- **Without an ad SDK:** Data Not Collected. Progress is saved only on the device.
- **With ads (LevelPlay or Unity Ads):**
  - Identifiers (Device ID): used for third-party advertising and analytics.
  - Usage Data (product interaction, advertising data).
  - Diagnostics.
  - Answer "Yes" to tracking only if you show the App Tracking Transparency prompt.
- **With in-app purchases:** Purchases (purchase history) is collected by Apple, not by the game. Declare it as "not linked to you" unless you add accounts.
