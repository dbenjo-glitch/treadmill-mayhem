[Your name / studio name] ("we") makes Treadmill Mayhem (the "Game"). This policy explains what information the Game handles.

## Information stored on your device
The Game saves your progress on your device only:
- coins and gears
- upgrades and unlocked characters
- achievements and settings
- the names you type into the local leaderboard

We do not receive this information. Deleting the Game deletes it.

## In-app purchases
Purchases are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your payment details. The Game receives only a confirmation of which item was bought, so it can deliver that item.

## Advertising
*Delete this section if you ship without ads.*

The Game shows ads through [LevelPlay / Unity Ads]. The ad provider may collect:
- your device's advertising identifier
- your approximate location (from your IP address)
- information about ad interactions

It uses this to show and measure ads. On iOS, the Game asks your permission before allowing tracking (App Tracking Transparency). You can change your choice at any time in Settings ▸ Privacy ▸ Tracking.
